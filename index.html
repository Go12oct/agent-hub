Set up in two minutes

Windows 10 or 11 and an Android phone. Nothing else to install.

  1. Install on your PCDownload agent-hub-windows.zip, unzip it and double-click Install-AgentHub.cmd. It starts with Windows and adds a tray icon. No admin rights needed.
  2. Install the phone appDownload agent-hub-android.apk on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  3. PairRight-click the tray icon, choose Pair phone, and scan the QR code with the app.
  4. Optional: alerts and remote accessAdd a private ntfy topic for notifications, and Tailscale to reach your PC away from home. See the README.

Questions

Is my code uploaded anywhere?

No. Agent Hub reads chats on your PC and sends them straight to your paired phone. Notifications carry only a one-line summary.

Why not the Play Store?

To keep it free. The APK is signed and updates install over the previous version.

Mac, Linux or iPhone?

Not yet. Agent Hub is built for Windows and Android today.

Can it approve Devin's cards?

Devin has no public API for that, so the phone shows the card and opens it in Devin with one tap.
